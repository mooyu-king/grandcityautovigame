About Us

Meet City Playbook, an independent GTA VI guide site and home of the original Grand City Auto VI city sandbox project.

What we publish

City Playbook is an independent English-language guide to playing GTA VI. We organize where-to-go and how-to-do-it questions into scannable articles, short answers and labelled images. We also report on our own city sandbox, Grand City Auto VI: Market Shift.

How guides are made

Our guides draw on published support documentation and editorial coverage. We name each guide's sources and distinguish original illustrations from official game screenshots. Where a feature or button varies by version, start with the current prompts on your console.

Independence

City Playbook is an independent editorial project and is unaffiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. The guide subject and our independently developed game are distinct projects.
