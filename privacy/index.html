Privacy Policy

Read what City Playbook stores on your device and how we use analytics and email to maintain the guide site.

Reading and searching

You can read the guides without an account. The guide search runs in your browser and does not send your typed queries to us. Checklist selections use local browser storage. Your browser can delete this data using its site controls.

Site analytics

We use a Plausible analytics integration at data.1back.link to understand visits and page usage. Analytics requests may include ordinary network and browser information used to produce aggregated site reports. We do not attach your search text, checklist contents or email address to analytics events.

Installation and local game storage

If your browser supports installation, the site can be saved as a web app. A service worker can cache recent site pages and assets for offline reading. Market Shift is embedded from s.templeofthejackal.com and uses browser storage on the game host. Embedded and standalone progress may be stored separately by your browser. Use your browser’s site data controls to remove the caches, checklists or saves.

Contact by email

When you email us your email provider and our inbox handle the message. We use the information you send to respond and investigate guide corrections. You can ask us to remove your message using the email listed on Contact.
