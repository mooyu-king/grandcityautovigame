Inside Grand City Auto VI

Grand City Auto VI starts near the beach and sends you into a neighborhood where streets, parked cars and late shifts connect. The current game build gives you a single starting objective, space to explore and a choice of how to deal with the first road blockage.

Use the screenshot to read the layout before playing. In Grand City Auto VI, the minimap shows the nearby streets and the objective panel tells you what you are working toward. On the street, the colored markers help you connect those instructions with a truck, a turn or a destination.

Your first shift

Explore Sunset Strand

Grand City Auto VI starts near the waterfront. Use the streets, beach and marked landmarks to get your bearings. Looking at the minimap before leaving the first block makes it easier to return when you see a job marker or spot a ride that you want to try.

Change your ride

In Grand City Auto VI, getting into a parked vehicle changes your movement and control prompts. A truck makes sense for a job that involves moving another vehicle. For a quick tour, choose an available ride and take the wider streets first so you can get used to braking and turning.

Take on a local job

Look at the objective panel to see what Grand City Auto VI asks of you right now. The first chapter has more than one route. Later activities draw on the same habits: read the current objective, look for the marked destination and check the result before you move on.

Night Market Blockade

The opening Grand City Auto VI job gives you two ways to help the night market. The truck route puts you behind the wheel. The detour route sends you to two stops on a side street. Each has its own markers and prompts, so you can choose what to try from the start.

Clear the road with the truck

Grand City Auto VI places a yellow service truck near the starting area. Follow the truck marker, get in and bring it to the stalled car. Read the action prompt as you approach. After attaching the car, take it to the marked unloading bay and look for the objective to update. Use wide turns with the extra vehicle behind you.

Send traffic down the side street

The detour route in Grand City Auto VI gives you two sign locations to reach. Walk or drive to the highlighted spots and place a sign at each one. Check the current objective after the first sign so you know which marker is left. This path is a useful first choice if you are still getting comfortable with driving.

Grand City Auto VI questions

Where does Grand City Auto VI: Market Shift take place?

Grand City Auto VI opens around Sunset Strand, a beach district with roads, parked vehicles and a night market. Follow the waterfront to orient yourself, then use the minimap to see how the side streets connect. The in-game markers indicate the current chapter objective. The screenshot above shows the map and objective panel as they appear during a job.

What should I do first in Grand City Auto VI?

Start with Night Market Blockade. The opening objective shows a yellow service truck and tells you about the two detour signs. Pick the route you feel ready to try, then look at the feedback after each action. Grand City Auto VI gives you enough information to start with either route, and the illustrated overview above helps you compare the first moves.

How do I tell which controls apply in Grand City Auto VI?

Read the control hints after you change from walking to driving. On keyboard, WASD handles movement and steering. E is used for interaction and Space applies the brake while driving. The in-game prompts can change with context, such as when an action becomes available near a vehicle. Use the hints shown by your Grand City Auto VI build as the reference.

Can I explore without immediately taking a job?

You can look around the starting district before committing to a route. In Grand City Auto VI, trying a parked vehicle and returning to the starting road is a useful way to practice. Spend a moment learning the difference between turning, braking and getting out. When you are ready, return to the objective marker and choose how to clear the blockage.

What happens after the first Grand City Auto VI mission?

Market Shift follows the district beyond the night market. Its current local build includes a fifteen chapter story, coast races and taxi fares. These activities build on moving between objectives, choosing a suitable vehicle and taking the route that makes sense for the job. The opening chapter is a good moment to learn how the minimap, vehicle prompts and objective panel work together.

How can I recover if I lose track of an objective?

Read the active objective in Grand City Auto VI before heading toward another vehicle or sign. Use the map for direction, then look at the street in front of you to choose an approach that the current vehicle can take. The pause menu can help if you need to check keys or recover from a difficult position. Review any recovery prompt before confirming.
